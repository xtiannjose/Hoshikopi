## How to read it

- **P0** (high): breaks a core flow or the two-minute demo. Fix first.
- **P1** (medium): a real defect or WCAG 2.2 AA failure in a main flow, or a missing safety net. These become batches 7.7 onward.
- **P2** (low): a minor defect or small gap. Pick it up when a batch touches that area.
- **P3** (info): an observation, nit or idea. No action planned.
- **Findings** name every saved finding a backlog item merges. `area:N` is the Nth finding of that area in [findings-2026-11-10.json](findings-2026-11-10.json), with its verdict in [verdicts-2026-11-10.json](verdicts-2026-11-10.json). `ORD-04` style ids are in [2026-11-06-interim.md](2026-11-06-interim.md), with verdicts in [verdicts-2026-11-06.json](verdicts-2026-11-06.json). Those files keep the full evidence, so the interim file stays as the record behind this one.
- Severity, problem, fix and effort come from the checker's verdict (S is under half a day, M a day or two, L more), not the original reviewer's.
- Merging rule: a 2026-11-06 finding joins the 2026-11-10 finding it repeats; 2026-11-10 findings merge only when each names the other; the two reviewer passes of 2026-11-06 (ID1 and ID2, SEC1 and SEC2, UI1 and UI2) merge with each other.
